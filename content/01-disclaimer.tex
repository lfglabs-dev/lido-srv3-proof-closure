equivalence, oracle truthfulness, cryptographic correctness, governance
correctness, or exact proxy/storage refinement beyond the named assumptions.

\noindent Every selected target row is backed by a source-code cross-reference,
trust-boundary entry, Verity/Lean theorem, and rebuild command. These artifacts
are checked over the focused economic model; they are not full deployed-system
refinement proofs.
