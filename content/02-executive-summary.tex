\section{Executive summary}
\label{sec:executive-summary}

\begin{notebox}
\noteboxtitle{Question answered by this report}
Can the PR \#1811 accounting rules for deposits, top-ups, reserves, module
balances, and rewards be stated so a machine can check them in a focused
model? Yes, for the six signed P0 properties (SRV3-P1--P6) below, under the
assumptions in \cref{sec:trust-boundary}. Top-up and allocation-capacity
(SRV3-P8, P9) support those six. SRV3-P7 and P10--P15 are follow-on lanes,
not acceptance rows.
\end{notebox}

\noindent ``Executable'' here means: the target is listed, undischarged premises
have assumption IDs, and \texttt{make test} plus \texttt{make prove} rebuild
from a clean checkout.

The model tracks three stores:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Buffer: depositable ETH versus ETH reserved for withdrawals.
  \item Router: ETH pulled for deposits and validator balances across modules.
  \item Reward: which accepted balances may set rewards, fees, and recipients.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent An accepted report is oracle module-balance data that passed the
modeled SRv3 checks and may update accounting. The lane checks those modeled
transitions. It does not prove deployment, cryptography, governance, gas, or
consensus.

\begin{notebox}
\noteboxtitle{Proof versus test}
A test checks one example. A Lean theorem checks every value in its statement.
The model and assumptions say exactly what the local command checks.
\end{notebox}

\begin{quote}
On the focused SRv3 economic state machine, the Verity/Lean model preserves
deposit and top-up conservation, reserve separation, module-balance
conservation, allocation-capacity bounds, report-before-reward consistency,
reward/fee bounds, and status gating, under the named assumptions.
\end{quote}

\noindent That is SRV3-P1--P6. Cryptographic witnesses, consensus, governance,
and deployment sit in \cref{sec:trust-boundary}.

Each matrix row names a transition, states the property, and is checked by
Lake against the listed assumptions.

\subsection*{Proof matrix --- six P0 candidate economic properties}

\noindent The six rows below are the signed P0 candidate economic-conservation
scope of the pilot. All have executable artifacts in \path{proofs/},
\path{LidoSRv3/}, and \path{verity/targets/}.

\noindent

\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.06}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.12\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.24\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \thead{ID} & \thead{PR \#1811 surface} & \thead{Executable property} \\
  \midrule
  SRV3-P1 & Lido buffer allocation &
  Withdrawal-reserved ETH is excluded from the depositable resource, so deposit
  spending cannot borrow from withdrawal liquidity. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P2 & Router deposit path &
  Router-pulled ETH equals 32 ETH times actual deposits and is fully sunk
  into CL deposits after requiring enough depositable ETH and reducing modeled
  buffered ETH by that exact amount; allocated-deposit sums match actual
  deposits after an existing active module passes the nonzero-capacity and
  max-deposit guards. The selected module's last-deposit field records that
  exact pulled amount through an exact module-array update while preserving
  module-array length and module-balance sums, with no modeled residual held by
  the router, no withdrawal-reserve field mutation, a deposit-reserve field
  reduced by exactly that pulled amount, and no router report-state mutation.
  Zero-deposit returns still record last-deposit accounting but leave reserves,
  modeled buffer, and beacon-sink value unmoved. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P3 & Module balances &
  Router-wide validator balance is exactly the sum of accepted per-module
  balances, the post-state module array is exactly the accepted report
  update-loop result, the successful report loop preserves module-array length
  and ETH-side state, and the exact accepted report is stored for later reward
  reads. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P4 & Oracle report ordering &
  Module fee-distribution weights come from the current accepted module-balance
  state, not from stale or partially applied reports. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P5 & Rewards and fees &
  Reward distribution returns empty rows and an empty module-id projection when
  total balance is zero; otherwise rows use accepted nonzero module balances,
  skip zero-balance modules, keep recipients aligned, bound paid module fees,
  and pay stopped modules zero module-side fee, with total fee equal to the
  row-wise module plus treasury fee sum. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P6 & Status gates &
  Non-active or deposit-paused modules receive no modeled deposit allocation;
  stopped modules receive no module-side reward or fee allocation. \\
  \bottomrule

\subsection*{Proof matrix --- internal economic decomposition}

\noindent The two rows below decompose the deposit and allocation surface of the
P0 scope into finer executable target groups. They are checked in the pinned
model and shown for completeness; they support the six P0 candidate properties
rather than adding separate signed acceptance rows.

  \toprule
  \thead{ID} & \thead{PR \#1811 surface} & \thead{Executable property} \\
  \midrule
  SRV3-P8 & Router top-up path &
  Top-up allocations are same-length, Gwei-aligned, within per-key limits and
  the capped, rounded module target, which keeps returned sums inside both the
  module allocation budget and the router-global per-block top-up cap; a
  zero-target queue advancement requires the Lido protocol deposit gate to be
  open. Nonzero sums require enough depositable ETH before the
  modeled Lido pull, reduce modeled buffered ETH by that exact sum, reduce the
  deposit-reserve field by exactly that sum, and are fully sunk; zero sums
  return the unchanged state. Both branches leave no modeled router residual and
  no withdrawal-reserve field mutation, and do not mutate router module or
  report accounting. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P9 & Deposit allocation capacity &
  SRv3 allocation-capacity rows preserve router module length and module-id
  order, and derived capacity values preserve router module length; active
  capacity is bounded by target share and available capacity, while inactive
  modules keep current allocation. \\

\subsection*{Review posture}

A protocol reviewer can follow the stores and the trust boundary without
code. A formal-methods reviewer can check target IDs, assumptions, and rebuild
gates against the local claim.
