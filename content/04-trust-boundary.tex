\section{Trust boundary}
\label{sec:trust-boundary}

The report proves SRv3 accounting invariants over the focused economic state
machine. It does not claim full deployed-system equivalence. This is intentional:
the proof is valuable because the boundary is short, named, and reviewable.

\subsection*{Named assumptions}

\noindent
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.27\linewidth}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \thead{ID} & \thead{Assumption} & \thead{Meaning} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  A-EXT-01 & External contract summaries &
  Lido buffer withdrawals, staking module callbacks, and fee recipients conform
  to the specified interfaces and mutate no unlisted SRv3 accounting state. \\
  \rowsep
  A-DEP-02 & Beacon deposit sink &
  A modeled beacon deposit of \(n\) validators removes exactly
  \(n \times 32\,ETH\) from the router-side deposit resource. \\
  \rowsep
  A-ORC-03 & Accepted oracle reports &
  Once accepted by SRv3 validation, module-balance reports satisfy the listed
  shape, alignment, freshness, and range assumptions. Consensus-layer truthfulness
  is outside this pilot. \\
  \rowsep
  A-ID-04 & Module identity well-formedness &
  Module IDs are unique in the modeled arrays, and report arrays align with those
  IDs where the Solidity path requires alignment. \\
  \rowsep
  A-ARITH-05 & Unit conversion domain &
  Wei/Gwei and basis-point arithmetic is interpreted over the bounded unsigned
  integer domains used by the modeled transitions. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}

\subsection*{Explicit non-claims}

\noindent
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.34\linewidth}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \thead{Boundary} & \thead{Treatment} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  BLS signatures, deposit roots, SSZ, Merkle proofs, EIP-4788 &
  Named cryptographic and consensus-data boundaries, not part of the P0
  accounting closure. \\
  \rowsep
  Exact packed-storage migration and proxy mechanics &
  Deferred to a deployment and migration lane; not used as proof evidence here. \\
  \rowsep
  Full governance role graph and parameter admissibility &
  Configuration is assumed to satisfy the stated range and role assumptions. \\
  \rowsep
  ExitBus, consolidation, top-up CL witness binding, VaultHub &
  Recommended follow-on proof lanes, deliberately outside the fixed four-week
  pilot. \\
  \rowsep
  Gas, events, revert strings, and exact call-stack shape &
  Engineering and integration review surfaces, not conservation-law premises. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}

\begin{notebox}
\noteboxtitle{Public wording}
The safe external sentence is: ``The focused SRv3 economic-state-machine model
proved the selected P0 accounting properties under named assumptions.'' Avoid
phrases such as ``SRv3 is fully verified'' or ``oracle and deposit correctness are
proved.''
\end{notebox}
