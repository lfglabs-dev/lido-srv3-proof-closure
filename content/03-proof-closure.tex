\subsection*{Evidence architecture}

\noindent
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.20\linewidth}p{0.20\linewidth}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \thead{Layer} & \thead{Artifact} & \thead{Role in the proof} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  Solidity-facing summaries & \path{verity/SRv3/*.vy} &
  Preserves the relevant array, loop, and call structure while summarizing
  external contracts through explicit interfaces. \\
  \rowsep
  Economic model & \path{lean/SRv3/State.lean} &
  Defines modules, reserves, Wei/Gwei arithmetic, status flags, accepted reports,
  rewards, and fee recipients. \\
  \rowsep
  Proof obligations & \path{lean/SRv3/Properties/*.lean} &
  States each P0 property as a theorem over arbitrary finite module and report
  arrays, with alignment and uniqueness assumptions named in the theorem
  signature. \\
  \rowsep
  Trust report & \path{reports/trust-boundary.json} &
  Machine-readable list of assumptions, boundaries, theorem IDs, axiom output,
  and source-to-property links. \\
  \rowsep
  Rebuild gate & \path{lake build SRv3Proofs} &
  Re-checks all theorem files, regenerates the proof register, and fails on any
  missing P0 theorem. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}

\subsection*{Theorem register}

\noindent
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.17\linewidth}p{0.31\linewidth}p{0.14\linewidth}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \thead{ID} & \thead{Theorem} & \thead{Status} & \thead{Evidence note} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  SRV3-P0-01 & \path{routerBalance_eq_sum_moduleBalances} &
  \cellcolor{sevoktint}Proved &
  Induction over accepted module report array; unique module IDs and aligned
  report lengths are explicit premises. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P0-02 & \path{deposit_spend_exact_32eth_multiple} &
  \cellcolor{sevoktint}Proved &
  Integer arithmetic proof over Wei, Gwei, and deposit-count conversions; rejects
  over-allocation before value leaves the modeled router. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P0-03 & \path{withdrawal_reserve_not_deposit_spendable} &
  \cellcolor{sevoktint}Proved &
  Separates available deposit reserve from withdrawal-reserved ETH in every
  deposit and top-up transition. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P0-04 & \path{rewards_bounded_and_aligned} &
  \cellcolor{sevoktint}Proved &
  Shows module, recipient, and amount arrays remain aligned and that computed
  fee sums do not exceed the valid reward basis. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P0-05 & \path{reward_report_freshness_required} &
  \cellcolor{sevoktint}Proved &
  Reward transition requires the most recent accepted report epoch and rejects
  internally inconsistent balance state. \\
  \rowsep
  SRV3-P0-06 & \path{stopped_modules_excluded_from_value_flows} &
  \cellcolor{sevoktint}Proved &
  Covers active, stopped, and deposits-paused gates for deposits, top-ups, and
  module-fee receipt. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}
