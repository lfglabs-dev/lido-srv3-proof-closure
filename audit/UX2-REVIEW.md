# UX2 review: per-guarantee display records against the documented Lido inputs

UX1 (#229) made the model-vs-deployed boundary and the open fidelity gaps
unmissable on the published surfaces. UX2 makes every canonical guarantee
individually displayable: `audit/ux2/<ID>.json` carries, for one guarantee at a
time, the registry wording, the two registered theorems with their role, plane,
exact Lean statement, file and line span, the assumptions with their recorded
risk, the open fidelity gaps, the pinned source spans, and the boundary. The
records are generated by `scripts/generate_ux2.py` from checked-in inputs only
and are re-derived by `make test`, so a record can never say more than
`audit/guarantees.yaml` and the Lean sources say.

## What UX2 does not do

- It generates no prose beyond the registry: plain-English and mathematical
  renderings live with the consumer (the marketing page) and must cite these
  records rather than restate them.
- It registers nothing and changes no Lean statement; the Lean input tree is
  identical to the branch it stacks on.
- It does not read the supplemental rows: they are not individually displayed
  on the report page, and their evidence stays in `audit/R1-FINAL-AUDITOR-REPORT.md`.
