Off-chain actors · they pick when & what: contracts bound how much

Depositor Bot
collects guardian signatures, picks module & timing, builds top-up Merkle proofs. Compromise = liveness problem, not funds.
Oracle daemons ×9
compute reports on a shared reference slot: deterministic and replayable by anyone.
Node operators
hold signing keys only; withdrawal credentials of every Lido validator point to the WithdrawalVault, so principal is out of reach.
